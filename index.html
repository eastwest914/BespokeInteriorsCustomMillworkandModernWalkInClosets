About Anna Gratia Studia | Modern walk-in closets and wardrobes

Anna Gratia Studia is a Brooklyn studio specializing in modern walk-in closets, wardrobes, custom cabinetry and bespoke interiors. We combine interior design thinking with hands-on manufacturing, so the ideas on the drawing board can be built exactly as drawn.

Our work covers everything from a single wardrobe to a full set of built-in furniture. We collaborate directly with homeowners and also with interior designers and architects who need a reliable custom millwork partner.

What Sets Us Apart

A design-led process, realistic 3D previews and careful production. You will understand the plan, the materials and the cost well before work begins.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer interior design consultations?

Yes. Our consultations cover layout, materials, storage planning and style, and they can be for a single closet or a larger interior project.


What is 3D interior visualization?

It is a realistic rendering of your design that shows proportions, finishes and lighting, so you can approve the look before manufacturing begins.


Do you work with designers and architects?

We do. Many projects come through design professionals who need custom millwork, cabinetry or closet systems built to specification.


What payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash and credit cards, and the cost is discussed with you before production starts.

Walk-In Closet Design

A walk-in closet should feel calm and effortless. We plan the flow of the room first, then place hanging, shelving, drawers and shoe storage where your hands expect them.

Lighting, mirrors and materials are chosen as part of the same design, so the space looks refined and works well day after day. You review everything in a 3D visual before we build.

Custom Wardrobes

Our wardrobes are designed around the room they live in. Door style, handle detail, interior fittings and color are all chosen to complement the architecture and your personal taste.

From clean handleless fronts to warm wood grains, each wardrobe is manufactured to your dimensions and installed cleanly, with every drawer and rod placed for easy daily use.

Custom Millwork and Cabinetry

Millwork is where design becomes detail. We produce cabinetry, paneling, shelving and trim that line up with your walls and with each other, with neat joints and consistent finishes.

Whether it is a single cabinet or a full set of rooms, we work from approved drawings and keep you informed through production, delivery and installation.

Built-In Furniture

Built-in furniture makes better use of a room than freestanding pieces. Shelving walls, home office units and window seats can be shaped to the room and finished to match.

We handle the design, manufacturing and installation, so everything fits tightly and looks intentional. It is a particularly good solution for Brooklyn homes with awkward corners.

Interior Design Consultation

A consultation is the best place to start if you are unsure how your space should work. We talk through your goals, review photos and measurements and suggest practical, stylish options.

You leave with a clear direction on layout, finishes and budget, and you can decide whether to continue with a full design and manufacturing package.

3D Interior Visualization

Floor plans are hard to read, and samples only show part of the picture. A 3D visualization shows proportion, color, texture and light in one image, so decisions feel easy and confident.

We can adjust finishes, handles and layouts on screen until the design is right. It reduces surprises and helps everyone involved, including designers and contractors, stay aligned.
